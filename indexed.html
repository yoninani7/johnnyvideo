Inquiries

Common
Questions

?
What is your standard turnaround time? +

Typical delivery for commercial projects is 14–21 business days from wrap. Premium rush delivery in 7 days is available at a surcharge. We never compromise quality for speed.

Do you work internationally? +

Absolutely. We maintain offices in Addis Ababa, Dubai, and Los Angeles. We've produced work across East Africa, the Middle East, and Europe. We travel fully equipped.

What's included in a production package? +

All packages include concept development, pre-production, full production crew and equipment, and post-production (color, sound, VFX as needed). We operate as a complete in-house studio.

How do I initiate a project? +

Fill in the form below or email us directly. We'll schedule a discovery call within 48 hours. Most projects begin with a free 30-minute creative consultation.

Do you offer licensing for stock footage? +

Yes. Our Ethiopia and East Africa aerial library is available for licensing. Contact us with your specific requirements and intended use case.
