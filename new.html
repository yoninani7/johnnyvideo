Inquiries

Common Questions

What is your turnaround time?
+

Typical delivery for commercial projects is 14–21 days post-production. Music videos and short films vary from 3–8 weeks depending on scope and post complexity.

Do you work internationally?
+

Yes, we operate full production units in Addis Ababa, Dubai, and Los Angeles. We also travel for projects globally — from Europe to Southeast Asia.

What is your minimum budget?
+

We work across a range of project sizes, but our minimum for commercial engagements starts at $5,000 USD. We believe in transparent pricing and will provide a full breakdown at the consultation stage.

Can I book you for a wedding?
+

Absolutely. Our wedding cinema packages are among our most sought-after services. We shoot with the same cinematic standards as our commercial productions — no compromise on your most important day.

What formats do you deliver in?
+

We deliver in any format you need — 4K/8K ProRes, H.264/H.265, DCP for film screenings, and optimized exports for social media. RAW files are available on certain packages.
